## The finding

**#39's pass gap is broken error recovery**, and it came out of this sweep's
reading of ds4#999 applied to logs we already had committed.

Malformed tool calls occur at the **same rate on both arms** of the #39 A/B —
19, 20, 21, 18 per 15-trial sweep — so they are a baseline property of Qwen on
ds4 under agent traffic, not an MTP effect. ds4#999 says why: Qwen tool-call
structure decodes at the request temperature (OpenCode's default is 1.0) with
no greedy protection.

What differs is recovery. The control arm recovers from every one; the string
`recovery` does not appear in either control log. The MTP arm fails on all of
them — 22 and 20 — with
`error="invalid tool call recovery failed: metal Qwen prefill failed at position N"`.

**All 20 of 20 distinct prefill-failure positions are frontier-short
positions.** Not correlated: identical. The recovery re-prefill trips the MTP
history frontier that the peer's temperature gate leaves stale.

Both mechanisms I published this morning for that gap — the separate KV
directory and the graph allocation — are wrong. Posted on #39.
