## Family verdicts

### 1. Qwen — no newer release

Nothing newer than **Qwen3.8-Flash-Next** (also called Qwen3.8-Flash) showed up.

- `@Alibaba_Qwen` last model posts (2026-08-26 to 2026-08-28) are still Flash-Next. No Qwen4, Qwen3.9, or Flash-Next-2.
- Practitioners still treat Flash-Next as current. `@ivanfioravanti` (2026-09-09) talks about `qwen4-exp` vs a future final Qwen4. That means Qwen4 is not out.
- Qwen3.8-27B GGUF is a dense sibling from **before** Flash-Next (Unsloth: 2026-08-14). It is not a successor.

GGUF Q2–Q4 on ~128 GB, Apple Metal (llama.cpp / ds4): **not applicable.** There is no newer model.

### 2. GLM / Zhipu — no newer release

Nothing newer than **GLM-5.3-Flash** (Ox Alpha, 2026-08-26).

- `@Zai_org` last model posts: Flash on 2026-08-26, then **GLM-5.3 flagship weights** on 2026-08-28. That flagship is the larger sibling (753B / 55B active), not a model after Flash.
- Z.ai docs still list Flash as the latest Flash-class model (checked 2026-09-09).
- One rumor post (`@defaiscope`, 2026-09-07) claimed GLM-6 in late September. That is rumor, not a release. No weights, no GGUF.

GGUF Q2–Q4 on ~128 GB, Apple Metal: **not applicable** for a successor. Existing Flash GGUFs (Unsloth 3-bit for 128 GB; ds4 Q2 on M5 Max 128 GB) are the current Flash, not a new family member.

### 3. DeepSeek — yes: V4.1-Flash, today

**DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash** shipped 2026-09-10T06:10:09Z. Open weights, MIT, [huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash).
